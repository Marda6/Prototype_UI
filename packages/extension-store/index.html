RobotReachExtension
Robot reach zone
Utility v0.1.1 EncySoftware 1.4k
Shows the robot reachable area as a dot cloud right in the ENCY graphics window.
1 / 1
About

Shows where the robot can actually get, as a cloud of small dots drawn right in the ENCY graphics window. The part, the robot and the reachable area are on screen at the same time, so “does the arm reach this face” stops being a guess.

Using it

Robot reach zone opens a small panel that stays on top of ENCY while you keep working. It has everything the feature needs and nothing else:

  • Tool orientation — what has to hold at a point for it to count as reachable:
    • Free — the tool tip only has to get there, in any orientation. The most generous answer, and the slowest: several directions are tried per point.
    • Tool vector — the tool axis it is in right now is kept, spin around it is free. The milling case.
    • Strict — the whole current tool frame is kept. The gripper case, and the strictest answer.
  • Point density — how fine the cloud is. The step in millimetres follows from the workspace the robot turns out to have, which is why the slider is a density and not a distance.
  • Build — scans and draws.
